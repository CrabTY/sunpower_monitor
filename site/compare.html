比较说明与来源 · 2026-09-29Notes & sources · 2026-09-29

价格为美元，税费、地区与调价以供应商为准。“未列出”指查阅资料未提及，不能据此认定不支持。Sunflower 的提醒形式和默认配置未单独验证；HA 的手机 / 邮件提醒默认未启用。Prices in USD; taxes, region and changes depend on the provider. “Not documented” means absent from reviewed docs, not proof of no support. Sunflower alert presentation and defaults were not independently tested; HA phone / email notifications are disabled by default.

HA 的历史由 Recorder 按实体状态变化记录，轮询间隔不等于固定历史间隔；PVS6 可选 1 秒实时流，实际留存还取决于 Recorder 配置。电池能源面板未被上游完整验证；Recorder 默认详细历史保留 10 天，长期统计取决于实体支持。HA Recorder saves entity state changes, rather than fixed-interval samples. PVS6 offers an optional 1-second live stream; retention also depends on Recorder configuration. Battery Energy Dashboard setup is not fully verified upstream; detailed history defaults to 10 days, with long-term statistics depending on entity support.
