How calibration works

PVS grid reading ÷ utility grid reading. At 46.5%, grid power is about 2.15× the PVS reading. Home use changes by the same correction. The saved ratio applies to past and future Live and History estimates after reload; 100% removes it.

Location and privacy details

Your city or ZIP is sent to a geocoding provider. Only confirmed approximate coordinates and time zone are stored, never the text you typed. Changing a confirmed location recalculates weather and daylight for history, so you'll confirm before replacing it.
